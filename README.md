# Wireless Mouse Battery Tray Icon

Shows your wireless mouse battery as a number in the Windows taskbar.

## Install

1. Download `WirelessMouseBatteryTray.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/racclololz/Wireless-Mouse-Battery-Tray-Icon/releases/latest).
2. Double-click it and click **Yes**. It starts with Windows from then on.
3. If a setup window opens, click the left and then the right button of your mouse in it, then click **Save**.

To update, do the same with the new version.

## Uninstall

1. Open **Settings > Apps > Installed apps**.
2. Find **Wireless Mouse Battery Tray**, click **...** and then **Uninstall**.

## Icon colors

- Green, yellow, red: 50-100%, 20-49%, 0-19%.
- Blue: charging or plugged in.
- Grey: mouse off or asleep (last known value).

Hover over the icon for details. Right-click it for options, including a low battery alert.

## Supported mice

- Logitech (Unifying, Bolt, Lightspeed, cable, Bluetooth)
- Endgame Gear wireless mice
- Bluetooth mice that show their battery in Windows
- Other mice that report battery the standard way

If your mouse does not work, right-click the icon, click **Create report...** and send the file from your Desktop to the developer.
